# Zura — Promo Reel Brief (for HeyGen)

**Format:** vertical 9:16, 1080×1920, 30 fps. **Length:** 28–32 seconds.
**Platforms:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, Threads.
**Images:** the `images/` folder. Use the `*_9x16.png` files, in numeric order. The `*_raw.png` files are the same screens without the frame, in case a full-screen close-up is needed.
All names and photos in the images are fictional (demo data). Do not add any real names, numbers or faces.

---

## STRICT RULES (follow exactly)

1. **Do not change, redraw or "improve" the app screens.** Show them exactly as provided: no AI regeneration, no new buttons, no text changes inside the screens, no stretching. Allowed: slow zoom (max 108%), pan, crossfade, slide, soft blur on the background only.
2. **No avatar on screen** unless I ask for one. If an avatar is used, it must be small, in a corner, and never cover the phone screen.
3. **Only the claims in this script.** Do not add "unhackable", "military-grade", "more secure than Signal/WhatsApp", "100% private", "AI", or any comparison with other apps. Do not invent features.
4. **Voice:** calm, confident, warm, natural. Not a shouting ad voice. Tempo ~150 words/min (English) / natural Gulf–Levant neutral Arabic (Modern Standard, clear pronunciation).
5. **Music:** minimal, modern, deep (ambient / soft electronic, ~90–100 BPM). It stays under the voice (−18 dB under speech). No vocals in the music. Licence-free.
6. **On-screen text:** large, bold, white, max 5 words per line, safe zone (keep 250 px clear at top and 350 px at bottom for platform UI). One text per scene, appearing with a short upward fade (200 ms).
7. **Colours:** deep night purple / indigo background (#1A1240 → #2D1B5A), accent violet #8B7CF6, gold #D4AF6A for the lock scene. No red except the "end call" button already in the images.
8. **Captions:** burn in subtitles of the voiceover (same language as the voice), centred, lower third, inside the safe zone.
9. **First 2 seconds must hook:** start directly on the lock screen (scene 1) with the first line. No logo intro, no black frames.
10. **End card** (last 3 s): app name "Zura" large, under it the tagline, plus "Android" small. No QR code, no store badges, no website (none exists yet).
